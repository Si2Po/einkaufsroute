About this app

Einkaufsroute · As of November 2026

A hobby project

Einkaufsroute is a private hobby project. The app is free, has no ads and is developed on the side.

It is 100 % “vibe-coded”: every line of code was written by Claude Code (with the AI model Claude Opus 5.5 by Anthropic), following my ideas and wishes.

You use it at your own risk and without any guarantee: there may be bugs, and the app may not exist forever. So save a backup now and then (Settings → Account and groups → Back up and transfer data).

Where your data is stored

  • On your device: The app stores everything in your device’s browser storage, so that it also works offline.
  • At Supabase (database, servers in the EU, Frankfurt), only if you have an account: your email address, your password (encrypted only), your username (if set), your lists, meals, stores, categories and the purchase history for the suggestions.
  • At GitHub Pages (GitHub, USA) the app itself is hosted. When loading it, GitHub technically sees your IP address, as with any website.

There is no tracking, no advertising, no analytics tools and no sharing with third parties.

Who sees what

  • What is in your group “Personal” is visible to you only.
  • What is in a shared group is visible to all members of that group. They see you with your username, or without one with the part of your email address before the @.
  • Whoever has a share link to a list sees exactly this one list, until the link is stopped.
  • The operator of this app could technically look into the database. This only happens if you ask for help, e.g. with a forgotten password.

Deleting your account

Under Settings → Account and groups → Delete account you delete your account yourself. This removes your email address, your password and everything in “Personal”. Shared groups remain for the other members; if you are the last member, the group is deleted as well.

You also have the right to access, correct and delete your data. Just write to the address below.

On a Pebble watch

There is a separate watch app for Pebble watches: github.com/Si2Po/einkaufsroute-pebble. It shows a list via its share link, checks items off and accepts dictation. It does not need an account.

  • The Pebble app on your phone stores the share link, along with the last loaded state of the list. The watch gets the list from the phone.
  • The phone only talks to the database at Supabase, just like the guest view in the browser.
  • When you dictate on the watch, the Pebble app’s speech service turns your recording into text. Einkaufsroute only receives this text.

Run it yourself

The code is open: github.com/Si2Po/einkaufsroute. With the instructions in the supabase folder anyone can set up their own copy for free (GitHub Pages + Supabase, both on the free plan). Then the data is only with you. (The instructions are in German.)
